# Support Policy

## Community Use

Annes Engine is open-source software provided under the MIT License. Downloading,
installing, evaluating, or using the software does not create a support
obligation for Quartet Labo LLC.

Public issues may be used to share reproducible defects, questions, and feature
requests. An issue does not guarantee a response, investigation, workaround,
fix, feature, release, or delivery date. Development and maintenance prioritize
contracted projects and customers with active support agreements.

## Paid Services

Quartet Labo LLC. separately offers paid services for applications built with
Annes Engine, including:

- requirements analysis and application design;
- installation, configuration, and deployment;
- customer-specific application development and integrations;
- data migration and upgrade assistance; and
- maintenance and technical support under a separate agreement.

The scope, fees, term, communication channel, and supported application and
versions are defined in the applicable order or support agreement.

### Standard Support

The initial Standard Support offering is a paid, best-effort service. Subject to
the applicable agreement, it may include:

- receiving inquiries about the contracted application and supported versions;
- initial triage and reasonable reproduction investigation;
- sharing known workarounds and technical information; and
- guidance about configuration, compatibility, and upgrades.

Standard Support does not include an SLA. It does not guarantee a response time,
recovery time, resolution deadline, uptime, a fix for every defect or security
issue, or delivery of a new release.

Unless separately agreed, Standard Support does not include feature development,
customer-specific changes, data recovery, changes made by the customer or a
third party, or resolution of issues caused by infrastructure, external
services, or dependencies.

The MIT License continues to govern the software itself. A paid agreement adds
only the services and obligations expressly stated in that agreement.
